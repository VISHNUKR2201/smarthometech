✦ OUR CORE VALUES

Engineered With Uncompromising Precision

We believe technology in the home should be invisible, intuitive, and respectful of your family’s privacy. Here are the core engineering standards that drive every Stackly installation.

Zero-Latency Local Automation

Even if the external internet goes offline, your switches, motion sensors, security alarms, and climate schedules continue executing instantaneously on local edge hubs.

Privacy-By-Design Philosophy

No eavesdropping, no telemetry selling, and no unauthorized cloud access. Your personal routines, voice interactions, and security feeds stay exclusively yours.

Proactive Machine Learning

Our automation engines self-tune based on ambient outdoor sunlight, room occupancy, temperature differentials, and household bedtime patterns.

Sustainable Energy Stewardship

Automated motorized shading and intelligent thermal modulation preserve HVAC energy, reducing your carbon footprint while enhancing daily comfort.
