✦ CONSULTATION FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our consultation procedures, on-site audits, and system handovers.

Is the initial architectural blueprint audit and consultation completely free?

Yes. We provide 100% complimentary initial consultations and preliminary CAD schematics reviews for homeowners, architects, and interior designers with no contractual obligation.

Do your field engineers conduct on-site audits outside of Salem?

Yes. Our certified automation engineering teams deploy across Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem) as well as nationwide for luxury estates and commercial developments.

What file formats should we provide for low-voltage wiring planning?

We accept AutoCAD (.DWG, .DXF), Revit (.RVT), high-resolution PDF architectural blueprints, or standard hand-drawn electrical layouts.

How quickly does your team respond to 24/7 emergency service requests?

Active SLA clients receive sub-15 minute remote telemetry diagnosis through our secure local tunnel, with on-site technician dispatch guaranteed within 2 to 4 hours if hardware replacement is required.

Require Urgent On-Site Field Assistance?

Our rapid-response automation engineers are on standby 24/7 for active smart homes, security systems, and high-availability installations.
