✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

Got questions regarding smart home deployment? Here are direct answers from our principal IoT engineering team.

Can you retrofit smart automation into existing properties without breaking walls?

Yes! While new constructions benefit from structured Cat6A/Fiber backbones, we deploy hybrid wireless mesh relays (Matter over Thread and Zigbee 3.0) directly behind existing electrical wall boxes without opening drywall.

What happens to my smart home if the external internet connection goes offline?

All core functions—including physical switches, keypad scenes, sensor automations, biometric door locks, and security alarms—continue running seamlessly on our local-first Edge OS without internet access.

Are my security camera feeds and voice recordings stored in the cloud?

No. We follow a strict Privacy-By-Design architecture. All video recordings and voice telemetry are processed locally on an encrypted on-premise NVR server protected by hardware AES-256 encryption.

How long does a full smart home deployment typically take?

Turnaround depends on scope: luxury apartments and retrofits are typically commissioned in 3 to 7 business days, while custom new-build villas align with the general contractor’s construction and pre-wire schedule.

What warranty and ongoing maintenance support does Stackly provide?

We provide a comprehensive 5-year hardware replacement warranty on core automation controllers, coupled with our 24/7 proactive SLA covering firmware updates, remote diagnostics, and on-site technician response.
